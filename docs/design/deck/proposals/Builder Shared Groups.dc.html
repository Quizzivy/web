A passage, a cloze or a recording, with its questions beside it

Teachers tell the owner that building questions on shared material is hard: nobody works out a cloze without being shown, and moving around a group and adding to it is confusing. This page redraws that work as one screen that looks like the student’s, with the material on one side, its questions on the other, and a gap made by selecting a word. Below the drawing it reads how the builder works today and names what gets in a teacher’s way. What is stored stays as it is, and so does every operation of the server but one: for the AI to suggest a gap’s wrong options, its operation has to read the gap’s sentence.

Start from what the teacher wants The outline offers a passage, a cloze and a recording by name.
  • A teacher thinks “a reading passage with five questions”, not “a shared-context group, then its shared content”. The button and its menu use their words and say in a line what each choice gives.
  • Each makes the same thing underneath: a group with its first material in place. The server has no kind to choose; the screen works the name out from what the group holds, as it does today.
  • So a pane started one way can become another: a recording can gain a text with gaps, and a passage can gain gaps or a recording.
Show what the student will see The material stays on screen while its questions are written.
  • The passage or the recording is the left half of the pane and the questions are the right half, the split the student gets. No dialog holds the material.
  • Questions open in place. A group’s questions are a stack of cards; one is open, the rest are a line each, and the next is added at the end of the stack.
Make the gap where the word is Select a word, press “Tạo chỗ trống”, and its question exists.
  • The word becomes the correct answer and the gap takes its question’s number. With wrong options typed, students choose; with none, they type the word.
  • Nothing is linked by hand. There is no list to pick a question from, so a gap cannot be left without one.
  • Canvas New Quizzes makes a blank the same way: the word is marked in the sentence, and each blank then says how it is answered.
  • The gap’s card can ask the AI for wrong options, with the panel the question editor has today. It is the one part that needs the contract to change.

What a teacher can build with it

Teachers who saw the first drawing said a recording can have a cloze too, and that there are more combinations than the three it offered. There are, because the model has no kinds: a group holds a text, a recording or both, and any text can have gaps. The menu’s choices are ways to start, and the pane lets one become another. This is what it makes, and the one thing it cannot, which teachers do not need.

What it would take to build

No migration, and every write is one the contract already has: a group made in the new pane opens in today’s builder and the other way round. One read needs a change, the AI’s wrong options for a gap, and that starts in the contract. The rest of the work is in the web. Teachers have seen the drawing; nobody has built anything with it yet.
