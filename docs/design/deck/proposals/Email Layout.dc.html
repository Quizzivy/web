One layout for every email

The deck draws no email. Four are sent today, as bare HTML with the centre's name, a few lines and a link, and fifteen more are planned for R7, R10 and R11. This page draws one layout that all nineteen fill, and seven of them in full: the invite that ships today and six of R7's. The layout is flat: one white panel on a soft ground, blocks set apart by space and a quieter ground, and no line or stripe anywhere. It keeps R5's rule: no images, no remote CSS and no tracking. Nothing here changes what an email is allowed to say.

{{ c }}
What does an email hold? One thing that happened, the facts, and one button into the app.
  • The centre's name, a heading, a greeting and one or two sentences. Then, where the email has them, the facts as a small table.
  • One button, and the same link written out under it. The app is where things are read and done.
  • A footer that says why the reader got it and where to turn it off.
Where is the brand? In the centre's name, and in how calm the page is. There is no logo.
  • R5 decided that emails carry no images, so nothing is blocked, nothing is fetched and nothing can track an open. A logo is an image, and the brand kit forbids setting the wordmark in type.
  • The colours are the deck's tokens written as hex, because mail clients read neither CSS variables nor oklch.
  • Nothing has a border: no outline on the panel, no coloured stripe on an edge, no bar beside a quote. The owner ruled edge stripes out for every surface on 12 Nov 2026.
  • The typeface is the reader's system face. Be Vietnam Pro would be a remote font.
Will it hold in real clients? It is one column that needs no media query to fit a phone.
  • 600px at most, fluid below. Built as tables with inline styles when it is implemented; the frames here show the result, not the markup.
  • The look follows the plain transactional emails of today: React Email's Barebone and Studio templates, and the receipts of Apple and Nike. A brand line, one large heading, a few lines, one button.
  • Dark is a second set of colours for clients that honour the reader's setting. A client that inverts colours itself still gets dark text on light and a solid button.
  • Every email keeps its plain-text part, drawn here beside the HTML.

Sizes and rules

Sizes are CSS pixels and do not change with the width. Body text is 16px so a phone does not have to zoom, and the button is 44px tall, the deck's floor for a touch target.
