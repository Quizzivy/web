Strict or flexible: how an assignment is taken, and what each side sees

A teacher asked for homework a student can stop and come back to. F1 gives every assignment a mode. Strict is the product as it is: a time limit from Start, and the teacher decides what happens when a student leaves the paper. Flexible has no time limit: the assignment’s close is the deadline, the close button is a break, and every absence is still recorded and the teacher told, with no action on the attempt. This page draws where the teacher chooses, what a student reads and sees under flexible, and the two times and the dated timeline a teacher reads afterwards. Each screen opens on the deck’s own frame, and every Vietnamese string that exists today is used as it is.
