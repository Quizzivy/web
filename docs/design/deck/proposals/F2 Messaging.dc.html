Messages for everyone at the centre

R7 let a staff member write first only to a student who is or was in their class. At a centre this small that was too narrow: the admin could not reach a student in a teacher’s class, and nobody could reach a student in no class. The owner decided on 23 Nov 2026 that every teacher and admin may write to any student, that a student still writes first only to a teacher of their own class, and that teachers may write to each other, to share material and experience. This page draws what that changes on R7’s Messages: a “Tin nhắn mới” button and the dialog it opens, a fourth tab for colleagues, a search that finds anyone, a thread with a student the caller does not teach, a thread between two colleagues, and what the student reads. Every screen opens on its R7 frame, “As today”, so the change sits beside it. The plan is docs/plan/77g-f2-messaging.md in Quizzivy/docs.
