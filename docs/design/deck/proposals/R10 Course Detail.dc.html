Course detail, in the parts the deck does not draw

The deck draws Course detail for a published course that belongs to the teacher looking at it: a heading with two buttons, units of lessons, and two cards at the side. R10 builds it (T-R10.13) with parts the drawing leaves out, and lists them for a drawing (DG-94). This page keeps the deck’s screen as it is and adds those parts, each from something the deck or the product already has: a draft course’s “Phát hành”, “Chia sẻ” and a “…” menu in the heading, a unit’s own menu to edit and delete it, a lesson being moved, a course shared with the teacher, a course with no unit, and the page loading and failing. Nothing here changes an operation.

{{ c }}
The heading The deck’s row of buttons, with the three it lacks.
  • A draft course has “Phát hành” where a published one has “Giao cho lớp”: a draft cannot be assigned. When no lesson is published yet, the button rests and one line beside it says why, as the bank’s Question editor does beside Save.
  • “Chia sẻ” opens the share dialog every other kind uses. It is the chip the deck draws on a course’s card.
  • “…” holds what is done rarely: “Sửa thông tin”, which is New course’s dialog with the course’s values, and “Xoá khoá học”.
A unit A unit has its own “…”, beside its release label.
  • “Sửa chương” is the deck’s Add unit dialog with the unit’s name and how it opens.
  • “Xoá chương” says what goes with it: its lessons, students’ progress on them, and the assignments of its test lessons, whose results stay.
  • A lesson is moved by its grip, with a pointer or with the keyboard, the way a question is moved in the test builder’s outline.
Someone else’s course The owner’s name, what the teacher may do, and only the buttons that work.
  • A shared course carries the line and the pill of R7’s shared tests: the owner, and “Có thể sửa” or “Có thể dùng”.
  • Can use reads, previews, assigns and copies. No grips, no menus, no “Thêm bài học”.
  • Can edit also changes units and lessons. Neither shares the course on, and neither deletes it.
