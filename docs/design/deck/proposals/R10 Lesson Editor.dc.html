The lesson editor, for the five kinds of lesson

The deck draws a course as units of lessons, and a lesson as a row: “Video · 6 min”, “Flashcards · 18 words”. Pressing a row opens nothing. R10 builds the screen behind it (T-R10.14) and asks for a drawing (DG-94). This page draws one, from parts the deck already has: the course’s heading, the question builder’s editor and media block, and the product’s upload row. A reading has a body and its minutes; a video and a listening have a file and notes; flashcards and a test point at a word list and a test. One footer saves, publishes and deletes. Nothing here changes an operation.

{{ c }}
One screen Five kinds, one shape.
  • A heading with the kind’s tile, the title and the status; the title field; what the kind needs; a footer. A teacher who has edited one kind knows the other four.
  • The kind is a label, not a field. It is chosen when the lesson is added, and the plan has no way to change it afterwards (Q2).
  • One column, at most 760 wide, so a body’s lines are about as long as on the student’s page, which is 720 wide.
Parts the deck has Nothing new to learn, and nothing new to build twice.
  • A body and notes are edited in the editor the question builder uses, with the same toolbar.
  • A video and an audio file use the builder’s media block: drop a file, choose one, or take one from Media. The limits are on the block before anything is chosen.
  • Progress and refusals are the product’s upload row and its sentences, with a video’s own limits: MP4, 95 MB, 30 minutes.
Saving Nothing saves by itself, and the footer says where things stand.
  • “Lưu” wakes when something has changed. Leaving with changes asks first.
  • “Phát hành” saves what is on the screen and publishes it. When the kind’s file, list or test is missing, the field says so and the lesson stays a draft.
  • Deleting asks, and says what students lose: their progress on the lesson, or for a test lesson that its assignment closes and results stay.
