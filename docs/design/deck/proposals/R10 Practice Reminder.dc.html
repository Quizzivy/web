The daily practice reminder, as an email and in the app

R10 reminds a student at 19:00 on a day with words to review and no practice yet (T-R10.6), in the app and by email. The deck draws the two switches behind it, the teacher’s in Assign flashcards and the student’s in Settings, and the Practice card on Home. It draws no email, and its bell has no practice item. This page draws the email in the layout every Quizzivy email uses (the Email Layout page), the item in the bell, and the student’s switch in the product’s own card, in Vietnamese and English. Nothing here changes an operation.

{{ c }}
The email One number and one button, in the layout every email uses.
  • It fills the Email Layout page’s panel and nothing more: the centre’s name, a heading, a greeting, two sentences, one button, the link in full, the footer.
  • The subject and the heading are the plan’s words: “{n} từ cần ôn hôm nay”. No list, no minutes and no streak; Home’s card has them.
  • The button opens Learn on its Flashcards tab, on the student’s host. The footer’s line leads to the switch that stops the email.
In the app The same sentence in the bell, and the deck’s switch in Settings.
  • The bell gets one more kind of item, with the flashcards’ icon. It opens the same place as the email’s button.
  • The student’s switch is the deck’s fourth row, in the card the product has today. One switch covers the app and email (DG-82).
  • The teacher’s switch, “Daily reminder at 19:00” in Assign flashcards, is the deck’s and is not drawn again.
When it comes Only on a day with words to review and no practice yet.
  • Four things must hold: the teacher kept the reminder on for a class that has the list, the student kept theirs on, words are due, and the student has not practised today.
  • Once a day, at 19:00. The item is there when the student opens the app after 19:00 in their time; the email leaves at 19:00 in the centre’s time.
  • A student who practises before 19:00 gets neither.
