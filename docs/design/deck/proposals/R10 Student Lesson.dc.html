The student’s lesson, for the kinds the deck does not draw

The deck draws a student’s Lesson page once, for a reading: a pill, the title, the body, and “Mark as done” pinned to the bottom. R10 builds the page for five kinds (T-R10.16) and lists four of them for a drawing (DG-94). This page keeps the deck’s Lesson exactly as it is and draws the rest inside it: a video and a listening with a player and the teacher’s notes, flashcards and a test with one card and one button, a lesson opened while it is locked, the last lesson of a course, and the page loading and failing. Nothing in the deck’s Learn, Course, Flashcards, Test intro or Result screens changes, and no operation changes.

The deck’s page One page, the deck’s, with a different middle.
  • The pill, the title, “Unit 2 · lesson 1 of 4”, the way back to the course and the button pinned to the bottom are the deck’s for every kind.
  • Only the middle changes: a reading’s body, a player with notes under it, or one card.
  • The page keeps the deck’s own width, 720px in the middle of the screen. The one page frame of the teacher’s workspace (DG-205) is not the student’s.
One button The button at the bottom is always the next thing to do.
  • “Đánh dấu đã học” for a reading, a video and a listening, as the deck has it.
  • “Luyện 18 từ này” for flashcards, “Bắt đầu làm bài” or “Tiếp tục làm bài” for a test: they open the deck’s Flashcards and Test screens, which bring the student back here.
  • Once the lesson is done the button is “Tiếp theo: …”, and on the course’s last lesson “Về khoá học”.
When it cannot be shown A reason and a way out, never a blank page.
  • A lesson opened by its address while it is locked says why, in the words the course’s page uses, and leads back to the course.
  • A video that will not play says so under the player, with “Thử lại”. The notes and the button stay.
  • Loading is the page’s own shape. A failed read is one sentence and “Thử lại”.
