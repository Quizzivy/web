Vocabulary, in the parts the deck does not draw

The deck draws Vocabulary and a word list for a teacher’s own lists: the cards, the heading with three buttons, the progress card, the table, and the dialogs to make a list, import one, add a word and assign flashcards. R10 builds them (T-R10.12) with parts the drawing leaves out. This page keeps the deck’s two screens as they are and adds those parts, each from something the deck or the product already has: where a word’s sound comes from (DG-95), what an import answers, “Chia sẻ” and a “…” menu on a list, a list shared with the teacher, and the pages empty, loading and failing. Nothing here changes an operation.

A word’s sound One audio file a word, added the way a question’s is.
  • “Phát âm” is the fifth field of the deck’s word dialog. It is the Question editor’s media field, for audio alone: drop a file, “Chọn tệp”, or “Từ Media”.
  • With no file, students hear the browser’s voice, and one line under the empty field says so.
  • With a file: its card, “Thay”, a button to take it off, and a player. The upload’s bar and its refusals are the product’s own.
An import The deck’s dialog, then the result the product’s own import shows.
  • The form is the deck’s: the columns, a file, “Dòng đầu là tiêu đề”.
  • The result is in the same dialog: what was added, what was skipped, and the rows that were refused with the reason for each, in the rows and the table of Admin › Users’ import.
  • “Mở danh sách” opens the new list. “Nhập tệp khác” starts again.
A list’s heading The deck’s three buttons, then “Chia sẻ” and “…”.
  • “…” holds “Sửa thông tin”, which is New word list’s dialog with the list’s name and level, and “Xoá danh sách”.
  • A list that came from a file has no level, and this dialog is where it gets one.
  • A shared list carries R7’s owner line, pill and band, and only the buttons that work.
