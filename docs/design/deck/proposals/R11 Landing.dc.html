The Landing for quizzivy.com

This is the first page a visitor to quizzivy.com sees. It says what Quizzivy is: the online learning system of an English centre, where students study lessons, review words and take tests, and teachers mark the work and follow each class. The top is one block of colour with a photo and brush strokes. Under it the page explains the system in four steps and three rows, each with a picture, then says who it is for and how tests are kept fair. The words were written for this page, Vietnamese first, and each line rests on something the product does. Under the frames is what the owner still has to decide, and a short list for the people who build it.

The look A photo, brush strokes, and a picture for each part.
  • The top of the page is a soft block of the brand’s lime. A person looks out from an arch, with three brush strokes behind and a few drawn marks around. Nothing from the product rests on the photo.
  • Each part has its own picture: five illustrations in the brand’s lime, and a photo for each kind of visitor.
  • The sections were laid out for this page: four steps on a card, three rows that change sides, three cards, a soft band, and one dark panel for the consultation.
The words About this system, in plain words.
  • The page says what a student does in Quizzivy and what a teacher sees, part by part. The copy table lists every line and what it rests on.
  • Nothing is made up: no count of learners, no awards, no customers’ logos, and no testimonials until the people named have agreed.
  • The phone is 036 321 6199 and the address is “TP. Hồ Chí Minh”.
The buttons Each one leads to the right place.
  • “Đăng nhập” asks one thing, student or teacher, and opens that sign-in.
  • A class code opens the class on the students’ site, as a teacher’s link does.
  • On a phone a menu holds the links, the two sign-ins, the language and the dark mode.

The photos and the pictures

Four free stock photos from StockSnap, each under CC0: free to use, with no credit needed. Five illustrations from unDraw, with its purple changed to the brand’s lime; unDraw’s licence lets a project use them, changed or not, at no cost and with no credit. The brush stroke, the sparkle, the squiggle and the line under the headline were drawn for this page. All of it is kept beside this page in proposals/landing/, with a list of where each came from. The people in the photos are not the centre’s students or staff. On the real page, photos of the centre’s own classes would say more, and need each person’s agreement.
