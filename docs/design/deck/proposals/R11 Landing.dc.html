The Landing for quizzivy.com

This is the first page a visitor to quizzivy.com sees. The top is laid out the way a landing page usually is: one warm block of colour, one photo of a person, a short headline, and the product shown in two small pieces beside the photo instead of a screenshot. The words are the deck’s own, Vietnamese first. Below the top the page follows the deck’s Landing, with a photo for each kind of visitor. Under the frames is what the owner still has to decide, and a short list for the people who build it.

The look One photo, one colour, few words.
  • The top of the page is a soft block of the brand’s lime, and the header sits on it. A person looks out from an arch with a lime circle behind.
  • Two pieces of the product rest on the photo: a test question and a marked result. Both are the deck’s own.
  • Under the top, a strip of four short facts stands where a template keeps its big numbers. Quizzivy has no numbers to show yet.
The words The deck’s, as they are.
  • Every headline, card and answer is the deck’s Vietnamese. One word changes, “Vào lớp” to “Tham gia lớp”, to match the app, and “Five question types” loses its number.
  • Nothing is made up: no count of learners, no awards, no customers’ logos, and no testimonials until the people named have agreed.
  • The photos are free stock photos. They stand in for the centre’s own.
The buttons Each one leads to the right place.
  • “Đăng nhập” asks one thing, student or teacher, and opens that sign-in.
  • A class code opens the class on the students’ site, as a teacher’s link does.
  • On a phone a menu holds the links, the two sign-ins, the language and the dark mode.
