The Landing for quizzivy.com, with the app on two hosts

The deck draws the Landing, and R11’s plan puts it on quizzivy.com. Both were made when the app was one host. Since v0.13.5 students are on my.quizzivy.com and staff on portal.quizzivy.com, each with its own sign-in, and quizzivy.com serves nothing yet. This page keeps the deck’s Landing, section by section, and settles what the two hosts change: where “Sign in” and the join box lead, what the page does for a visitor who is signed in, and which calls it makes. It also draws what the deck leaves out (the menu below 960, the join box’s refusals, the form’s consent and its states), and says only what the product has today. Each choice has a default, drawn in the frames, and is a question for the owner below.

The apex A page that leads to the two hosts. It signs nobody in.
  • “Đăng nhập” opens two choices: students go to my.quizzivy.com, teachers and admins to portal.quizzivy.com. Every link into the app is written in full, with its host.
  • The join box opens the code on the student host, where the class is looked up, as a teacher’s link does.
  • The page holds no session and reads no cookie. A visitor who is signed in sees the Landing like anyone else, and their host takes them in.
What it says Only what the product has on the day it opens.
  • Three of the deck’s six feature cards name modules that ship later (courses, flashcards, the gradebook). At launch their places go to what is there: imports from Word and PDF, the AI assistant, results.
  • Testimonials stay off the page until a real person has agreed to theirs, as R11’s plan decided. The deck’s three name real people.
  • Each release that ships a module puts the deck’s line back. The table below lists them.
What the deck leaves out The menu below 960, the join box’s refusals, the form’s consent and states.
  • Below 960 the section links go into a menu, with the two sign-ins. Below 720 the language and the theme go there too: the deck’s header is 406px wide on a 360 screen.
  • The join box is the product’s own field: it says why a code cannot be right before it leaves the page.
  • The form asks for consent, as DG-06 decided, and says what happened when a request cannot be sent.
