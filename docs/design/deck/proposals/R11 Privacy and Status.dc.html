The privacy notice and the status page

The Landing of quizzivy.com leads to two pages that nobody has drawn. The consultation form’s box to tick links to a privacy notice, and the footer links to a status page. This page draws both. The notice is written in plain words, Vietnamese first, from what R11’s plan says the form collects and keeps; three of the plan’s facts have gone out of date and are asked about below. The status page sits on the deck’s system frame and says one of five things. Under the frames is what the owner still has to decide, and a short list for the people who build it.

The privacy notice One column to read, four facts first.
  • It sits between the Landing’s header and footer. A title, one sentence, the version, and a button to print it.
  • A summary on a soft panel: what is received, what for, how long, and what the reader can ask for. Then eleven short numbered parts.
  • Every sentence says something R11’s plan says about the form. It has not had a legal review; that is the plan’s open item O-R11.1.
Three facts that moved The plan was written before them.
  • Where requests are kept. The plan names Fly.io and Neon. Since 12 November the server is in Ho Chi Minh City.
  • Which law. The plan names Decree 13/2023. A law on personal data has been in force since 1 January 2026, and that decree was replaced.
  • The version. The plan calls the first version 2026-10. Nothing was published then.
The status page The deck’s system frame, five states.
  • All normal, an update is planned, being updated, checking, and could not check.
  • “Being updated” is the app’s own maintenance page, with its words as they are today.
  • “All normal” needs a drawing the deck does not have. It is the deck’s maintenance drawing with its broken line made whole.
