What a recipient sees in a shared test

R7 lets a teacher open a test or a question that belongs to someone else, with Can edit or Can use. The deck says this in a toast and draws none of it. This page draws the five places it shows: the test builder, test detail, the Shared content dialog, the question editor and the assignment wizard's test step, for the owner, a Can-edit recipient and a Can-use recipient. It follows the question sets page: the same owner line, the same pill, the same sentence under the heading.

{{ c }}
How does a recipient know? The owner and the access sit under the title on every screen.
  • The owner's initials and name, then Shared with me's pill. It is the line the question sets page uses.
  • One sentence under the heading says what the access means here. For Can edit it is the deck's own: "Changes you make are saved to Đức's copy."
  • The owner's screens do not change, except that a refused save now names who saved first.
What is read-only? Everything that would change the test is left out, not greyed.
  • Can use has no drag grips, no row menus, no add buttons and no toolbar. Fields sit on the muted ground and keep their text at full contrast, because reading them is the point.
  • There is no save line, since nothing is saved.
  • What a Can-use recipient can do is in the title bar: Preview, Make a copy and Assign.
When two people edit? Three moments, each with its own notice.
  • Someone saved first: a warning band names them and the time, with Reload.
  • The question is used elsewhere: the change is saved as a new copy and a toast says why.
  • Sharing stopped: the builder gives way to one block with a way back to Shared with me.
