Shared with me, as R7 ships it

The deck draws Shared with me once: five rows of four kinds, in English, with everything loaded. R7 builds the screen (T-R7.17) with the two kinds it has, tests and question sets; word lists and courses come with R10. This page draws the screen as R7 ships it, in Vietnamese for the first time, and the states the plan asks for and the deck leaves out: loading, nothing shared, nothing matching, a list that cannot load, an owner whose account is disabled, the two confirms, and an item that is gone by the time the teacher acts on it. The layout, the columns and the three actions are the deck’s.

{{ c }}
What R7 has Two kinds now, and the page says only what exists.
  • Three tabs: “Tất cả”, “Đề thi”, “Câu hỏi”. The deck’s “Word lists” and “Courses” join them with R10 (70 §2 rule 5).
  • The sub-line and the empty sentence name tests and questions only, so a teacher is never told to expect a kind that cannot be shared yet.
  • A question set says its size on its kind line, “Bộ câu hỏi · 48 câu”. The deck writes the size into the name.
Whose it is Everything here still belongs to someone else, and the row says who.
  • The owner’s name is on every row. An owner whose account is disabled is flagged under the name, and what they shared keeps working (T-R7.8).
  • “Tạo bản sao” is the way to own it: the copy is the teacher’s, and nothing ties it to the original.
  • “Gỡ” is leaving, not deleting: the item stays its owner’s. The confirm says what stays and how to get it back.
When it does not work One sentence and one action, inside the card.
  • The header, the sentence about access, the tabs and the search stay through every state. Only the card changes.
  • A list that cannot load offers “Thử lại”; a search that matches nothing offers “Xoá bộ lọc”; an empty list offers nothing, because there is nothing to do.
  • An item that stopped being shared while the list was open answers with one toast, and the list reloads.
