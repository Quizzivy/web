Attendance, in the states the deck does not draw

The deck draws both Attendance screens in full: the page that lists sessions across classes, and the register in a class’s Attendance tab. Both are drawn with sessions to show and a register to take. R8’s plan names the rest (T-R8.12): the page and the register while they load and when they fail, a class with no session, and a session whose register cannot be taken because it has not started or was cancelled. This page draws those from the deck’s own parts, and puts both screens in Vietnamese for the first time. Nothing here changes an operation.

A register that cannot be taken The roster shows, and one line says why it is closed.
  • A session that has not started can be opened from a link. Its register lists who is expected, with every choice dimmed, and says when it opens.
  • A cancelled session reads the same way, with its own line. The chip beside the session picker says “Đã huỷ”.
  • In both, what cannot be used is not shown: no checkbox, no note, no “Đánh dấu tất cả có mặt”.
Nothing to list Each card of the page keeps its own sentence.
  • The page’s three cards stay when they are empty: the teacher sees that there is nothing today, nothing owed and nothing recent, each in its place.
  • “Hôm nay” says that no class has started, not that there is none: the card lists sessions only once they start (O-R8.6).
  • A class with no session at all gives one sentence in its Attendance tab and a link to the calendar, where a session is made.
Loading and failing The frame of the screen first; one sentence when it fails.
  • The page’s three cards and the class’s header and tabs are there at once. Blocks stand where rows will be.
  • A failed read gives one sentence and “Thử lại”, in place of the cards or of the register, and the rest of the screen stays.
  • A register being saved is not drawn: the deck draws its bar and its toast.
