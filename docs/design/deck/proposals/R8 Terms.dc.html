Terms, in System settings

R8 measures attendance “this term”, and R9’s gradebook is kept by term. A term is a name and two dates that an admin sets once for the whole centre. The deck has no screen for it (DG-93, DG-57); R8’s plan puts it in Admin › System settings as a fifth section and builds it from deck parts (T-R8.13). This page draws that section: the list, the dialog that adds and edits a term, the two ways a date can be refused, the confirm to delete, and the empty, loading and failed states. One sentence goes beyond the plan and is a question below: what the form says when a term’s end date is moved.

{{ c }}
Where it lives A fifth section of System settings, after “Tổ chức”.
  • The plan’s place and the plan’s icon. The other four sections are the product’s, untouched.
  • It saves itself: a dialog adds or changes one term and closes. The page’s unsaved-changes bar never shows for this section, so “Thêm học kỳ” sits in the card’s own header.
  • The card says in one line what a term is for, since nothing else on the Admin side mentions it.
The list A name, two dates, and where today falls.
  • Newest first, as the read returns them. A pill says “Đang diễn ra”, “Sắp tới” or “Đã qua”, worked out by the server in the admin’s time zone.
  • Each row has the product’s row menu, with “Sửa” and “Xoá”. On a phone a row becomes two lines and keeps its menu.
  • Terms may leave gaps between them and may not overlap. A day in no term counts attendance “overall”.
When a date is refused The form says which term is in the way.
  • An overlap is the server’s to find: it answers with the name of the other term, and the sentence sits under the two dates.
  • An end before the start is caught in the form, before anything is sent.
  • Deleting asks first, with the plan’s sentence: attendance stays, and the rates of that term are counted over every session.
