The student’s week, a class’s schedule, and a calendar with nothing to show

Three smaller things R8 builds and the deck draws only in part. The student’s “This week” is drawn full: this page adds a cancelled session, a week with nothing in it, and the loading and failed states (T-R8.15). A class’s schedule stops being a line a teacher types and comes from its sessions: this page draws the Schedule field of Edit class as it then reads (T-R8.14). And the teacher’s Calendar is drawn with a busy week: this page draws it loading, empty and failed (T-R8.10). Everything is in Vietnamese first, and nothing here changes an operation.

The student’s week A cancelled class stays in the week, and says so.
  • It is not removed: a student who expects a class on Thursday has to be told it is off, not left to wonder where it went.
  • It is muted, its name is struck through and its second line reads “Đã huỷ”, the same marks the teacher’s calendar uses.
  • A day with nothing reads “Không có lịch”. A week with nothing gives one sentence in place of seven.
A class’s schedule Once a class has sessions, its schedule is read from them.
  • The line on a class card, in class detail and in the Admin table is worked out from the sessions that repeat. Nobody types it.
  • In Edit class the field becomes read-only, with “Đổi trong Lịch” beside it and a line that says where it comes from.
  • A class with no session keeps the field it has today, so a teacher who has not used the calendar loses nothing.
Nothing to show The grid stays; a sentence sits on it.
  • An empty week keeps its days and hours, so the teacher still sees which week it is and can move on. The sentence and “Buổi học” sit over the grid.
  • While it loads, blocks stand where sessions will be. The week’s days are known from the address, so the header is there at once.
  • When it cannot load, one sentence and “Thử lại” take the grid’s place.
