The gradebook: which term, which class, a phone, and nothing to show

The deck draws the gradebook once: one term named in a sentence, four classes on tabs, eight students, six columns, at a desktop’s width. R9 builds it (T-R9.7) with things the drawing leaves out: a way to choose the term, a class picker when tabs do not fit, a phone, a cell’s tooltip, the row a teacher arrives at from Students, and the screen with no class, no work, still loading, or failed. This page draws those from the deck’s own parts, in Vietnamese first. The category and weight of a column are the page before this one.

{{ c }}
Which term A button in the heading, like the two on Reports.
  • The plan makes the term’s name a menu. The page puts it beside “Xuất” as the deck’s Reports heading puts its class and its range: a bordered button with a chevron.
  • The menu lists the centre’s terms, newest first, and “Tất cả học kỳ”. The sentence under the title still names the term and the two shares.
  • A centre with no terms has no button: the sentence reads “Tất cả học kỳ”, and there is nothing to choose.
Which class Tabs while they fit, one select when they do not.
  • Up to five classes are the deck’s tabs. From the sixth, and always below 768px, they are one select with the class’s name.
  • On a phone the student column gives up its avatar and half its width, so two grades show beside a name, not one. The table still scrolls sideways.
  • A teacher who comes from Students for one person lands on that person’s row, tinted, on the page that holds it.
Nothing to show The heading stays, and one sentence says what to do.
  • No class at all: “Tạo lớp để xem điểm.” and the way to make one.
  • A class with no work in the term keeps its tabs and its term button, so the teacher can look at another class or another term before assigning anything.
  • Loading is the table’s own shape. A failed read is one sentence and “Thử lại”.
