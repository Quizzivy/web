How an assignment counts in the gradebook, and where a teacher says so

The deck’s Gradebook reads “tests 60%, homework 40%” and gives every column a kind and a weight. Nothing in the deck sets them (DG-93). R9’s plan gives each assignment a category and a weight and names three places to set them: the wizard’s Schedule step, the assignment’s Settings tab, and the gradebook’s own column heading (T-R9.6, T-R9.7). This page draws the three, with one set of two controls used in all of them, in Vietnamese first. It follows the plan’s wording throughout, and says in its questions where that wording will mislead: a weight is shown as a percent, and it is one only while a term’s weights happen to add to a hundred.

Two controls, three places The same pair wherever it is set.
  • “Tính là”, a choice of two, and “Trọng số”, a stepper from 0 to 100 by fives. Both are parts the wizard already has.
  • In the wizard they are a group of their own under “Thời gian và số lượt”, and the summary beside the form gains one line.
  • In the assignment’s Settings tab and from a gradebook column they open in the dialog the Settings tab already uses, so a teacher learns one form.
While the assignment is live It can always be changed, and students are not told.
  • The other Settings cards lock while students are taking the test. This one does not: a weight changes no student’s test.
  • The toast after saving says only that it is saved. The Settings tab’s usual toast adds “Học viên thấy thay đổi ngay”, which is not true of a weight: students never see one.
  • A weight of 0 keeps the column in the gradebook and out of the final grade, and each place says so.
In the gradebook A column’s heading is the way in.
  • The plan makes the heading a button. The page gives it the look of one when the pointer is over it: the muted tint and a pencil.
  • After a save the heading’s shares are worked out again from every column’s weight in the term.
  • That is where a weight and a percent come apart: see Q1.
