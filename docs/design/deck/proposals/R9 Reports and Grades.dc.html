Reports and Grades, in the states the deck does not draw

Two screens R9 builds from full drawings. The teacher’s Reports is drawn with eight weeks of results and four students to check in with. The student’s Grades is drawn with six results and a tile that joins grammar and vocabulary. This page draws what R9 adds to each (T-R9.8, T-R9.9): a week with no result, a figure with no change, a range that cannot be chosen, twelve weeks on a phone, the cards with nothing, loading and failed; and for the student, one tile per skill, a result whose score is held back, more results to load, and the page with none. Both are in Vietnamese first, and nothing here changes an operation.

Reports, when a number is missing A gap is shown as a gap.
  • A week with no result has no bar and reads “—” above its place. The other weeks keep their heights.
  • A figure that did not change shows no arrow and no number beside it: an arrow at zero would say “up” or “down” about nothing.
  • “Học kỳ này” is in the range menu even with no term running, dimmed, with one line that says why.
Reports, with nothing to show One read fills the screen, and each card says what it lacks.
  • While the read is on its way, each card waits in its own shape. The heading, the two buttons and the cards’ titles are there at once.
  • With nothing handed in during the range, the average and the rate read “—”, the chart and the skills give the plan’s sentence, and the list has its own. “Học viên hoạt động” keeps its number: it is about this week, whatever the range.
  • When the read fails, the cards can only fail together. One sentence and one “Thử lại” stand where they were (Q2).
Grades, for the student One tile per skill, and never a score that is being held back.
  • The deck joins grammar and vocabulary in one tile. R9 shows each skill that has results on its own tile, after the average (DG-97).
  • A result whose score is not shown yet reads “Đã nộp”, as it does on Home, with no number and no bar. It is not in the average or in the tiles either, so nothing can be worked out from them.
  • “Xem thêm” brings the next results. With none at all, one sentence says where they will come from.
