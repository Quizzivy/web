# Photo and graphics of the landing site

Copied from the proposal page's folder, `docs/design/deck/proposals/landing/`
(its `CREDITS.md` is the fuller record). Keep this file beside the assets.

## Photo

`hero.jpg` stands in for the centre's own photo. It is from StockSnap, under
CC0 (free to use, no credit needed): "A young woman smiling at the camera" by
Matt Moloney, <https://stocksnap.io/photo/person-smiling-QH2V8JROLS>, cropped
and made smaller for the page.

The person in the photo is not a student or a teacher of the centre. A photo
from the centre's own classes takes its place once there is one, with that
person's agreement.

## Illustrations

`ill-listening.svg`, `ill-tests.svg`, `ill-results.svg`, `ill-fair.svg` and
`ill-faq.svg` are from unDraw (<https://undraw.co>, by Katerina Limpitsouni),
downloaded on 2026-11-14, with only the accent colour changed to the brand's
lime. unDraw's licence (<https://undraw.co/license>) lets a project use its
illustrations, changed or not, for free and without credit; it does not allow
passing them on as a set, building a service like unDraw from them, or
training models on them. Take any further ones from unDraw itself.

Their colours are drawn in, so each sits on a tile that stays light in the
dark theme (`.landing-tile`).

## Drawn for the page

`brush.svg`, `sparkle.svg`, `squiggle.svg` and `underline.svg` are shapes
only. The page uses each as a mask over a colour token (`Shape`), so they
follow the theme: never bake a colour into them.
